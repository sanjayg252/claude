# The Bucket Presentation - Voiceover Script

**Total Duration:** 2 minutes (120 seconds)

---

## How to Record Your Video

1. **Open** `index.html` in a browser (Chrome recommended for best performance)
2. **Use screen recording software** (OBS, Loom, QuickTime, or similar)
3. **Set browser to fullscreen** (F11 on most browsers)
4. **Click "Play"** to start the animation
5. **Read the script below** in sync with the on-screen cues
6. **Export** your recording at 1080p or higher

---

## Voiceover Script with Timing Cues

### [0:00 - 0:04] Title Screen
*[Pause - let the title breathe]*

---

### [0:04 - 0:12] Introducing the Bucket
> "Let me introduce you to **The Bucket** — a simple way to see your entire financial picture."

---

### [0:12 - 0:24] Inside the Bucket
> "Inside your bucket is **liquid money** — cash, savings, ISAs, shares. Anything you can access within seven to ten days."
>
> *[Pause as assets appear]*
>
> "This is YOUR money. You can spend it whenever you like."

---

### [0:24 - 0:36] Outside the Bucket
> "Outside your bucket sits everything else — your home, pension funds, business value."
>
> *[Pause as assets appear]*
>
> "This is still your money... but you can't spend it directly. You'd need to sell your home or draw from your pension first."

---

### [0:36 - 0:50] The Inflows
> "Above the bucket are your **inflows** — money coming in."
>
> *[Pause as items appear]*
>
> "Current income like salary and dividends... plus future income like your State Pension. Some of these will stop one day."

---

### [0:50 - 1:12] The Taps (Expenditure)
> "Below the bucket are the **taps** — where your money flows out."
>
> *[Pause as taps appear one by one]*
>
> "Your **current lifestyle** — the car, the holidays, the schools."
>
> "**Active retirement** — when you're fit enough to enjoy life most. Often when people want to spend more, not less."
>
> "**Late retirement** — when physical limitations mean spending typically decreases... though care costs may arise."
>
> "And **one-off events** — a child's wedding, a dream holiday, a new home."

---

### [1:12 - 1:27] The Key Question
> "Once we've agreed what's in your bucket, what's coming in, and what's going out..."
>
> *[Pause for emphasis]*
>
> "...we can answer the most important question: **What's going to happen to your bucket over your lifetime?**"

---

### [1:27 - 1:47] The Three Types
> "There are only three possibilities."
>
> *[As each type appears]*
>
> "You either **don't have enough** — your bucket will run dry."
>
> "You have **too much** — and might miss opportunities to live fully while you can."
>
> "Or you're **just right** — you have enough, but you don't realise it yet... so you stress, over-save, and under-live."

---

### [1:47 - 2:00] Closing
> "The bucket helps you see where you stand — so you can make decisions with **confidence**, not anxiety."
>
> *[Let final screen breathe]*
>
> "Because life... is not a rehearsal."

## Tips for a Professional Recording

### Voice
- Speak at a **calm, confident pace**
- **Pause** between sections to let visuals land
- Emphasize words in **bold** naturally
- Keep energy warm but professional

### Technical
- Record in a **quiet room**
- Use a decent microphone (even phone earbuds work better than laptop mic)
- Test audio levels before full recording
- Record at **1080p minimum** (4K if possible)

### Editing (Optional)
- Add subtle background music (royalty-free)
- Add your company logo at start/end
- Include contact details on final frame
